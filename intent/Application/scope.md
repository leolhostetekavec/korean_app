# Focused V1 Scope

This component protects the beginner experience from features that would complicate or displace the core recall loop.

## FocusedV1Contract

- binding: semantic
- artifact_path: crates/app/src/lib.rs

The V1 application must:

1. Provide no visible Hangul on learning, practice, or revision cards.
2. Provide no Hangul course, writing exercise, or grammar-heavy explanation.
3. Provide no speech recognition or pronunciation scoring.
4. Provide no AI-generated conversation.
5. Provide no dictionary interface or individual-word practice.
6. Provide no notifications.
7. Provide no user account, cloud synchronization, or social feature.
8. Provide no complex gamification.
9. Provide no automatically generated revision due dates or mandatory daily schedule.
10. Keep future extension points subordinate to the V1 recall interaction and invisible when unused.
